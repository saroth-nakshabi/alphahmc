# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, both arriving mostly from Google Ads on a phone:

- **Healthcare professionals** (doctors, nurses, pharmacists, dentists, allied health) who need a license from DHA, DOH or MOHAP in the UAE, or from SCFHS, DHP or OMSB elsewhere in the GCC. Many are still abroad, often in India. They want to know whether they are eligible, what the steps are, how long it takes and what it costs, then talk to someone on WhatsApp.
- **Investors and operators** opening, expanding or auditing a clinic, medical centre, hospital, pharmacy or drug store. They want to know what approvals they need, how the process runs and whether this team has done it before.

## Product Purpose

alphahmc.com is the public site of Alpha Health Group, a UAE healthcare consultancy. Its job is to answer the visitor's licensing or setup question clearly enough that they trust the team, then make the next step (WhatsApp, a consultation request) obvious. Success is a qualified inquiry, not time on page. Content is managed from the admin dashboard; pages render database content.

## Brand Personality

Expert, calm, direct. The voice of a senior regulatory adviser: confident and plain-spoken, precise about authorities, documents and steps, never hyped. Trust comes from specific, correct information and a clear next step, not from adjectives. No promises the team cannot keep, no invented figures, no claims that we make anyone "compliant".

## Anti-references

- **Big-4 corporate consultancy:** cold abstract stock imagery, jargon, long preambles before the answer, the sense that a human is hard to reach.
- By extension: pages that talk about "excellence" and "solutions" instead of answering the question the visitor searched for.

## Design Principles

1. **Answer first.** The page states what the visitor came to learn in its first lines and headings; detail follows.
2. **One obvious next step.** WhatsApp and a consultation request are always within reach, especially on the first screen of a phone.
3. **Precision is the polish.** Correct authority names, document names and steps do more for trust than decoration.
4. **Quiet over loud.** Restrained motion and effects; nothing competes with the content or slows a phone.
5. **The content lives in the database.** Templates render fields; copy changes go through the admin or the database, not Blade.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA, mobile-first: AA contrast for text and controls, full keyboard access, visible focus, labelled form fields with errors tied to inputs, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Most ad visitors are on phones over mobile data, so first-screen content and speed matter.
